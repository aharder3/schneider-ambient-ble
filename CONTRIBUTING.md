# Contributing

Contributions, protocol observations and fixes are welcome.

## Privacy requirements

Do not commit or post:

- credentials or secrets
- private network addresses tied to a real installation
- real Bluetooth MAC addresses unless strictly necessary and intentionally disclosed
- raw PacketLogger captures containing unrelated nearby-device metadata
- personal names, serial numbers or other identifiers

Prefer minimal, redacted ATT/GATT excerpts that demonstrate one controlled action.

## Development

Keep protocol findings in `docs/protocol.md`. Keep device-specific values out of public examples.
